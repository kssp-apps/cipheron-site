CipherON privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CipherON ("the app", "CipherON", "we") handles information when you use it. CipherON is an offline-first password manager for Android. "You" means the person using the app. "The developer" means the individual who publishes CipherON (see Contact).

2. The short version

The developer does not collect, receive, store or sell your personal data. The app keeps your vault on your phone, encrypted, under your control. The developer runs no servers for the app and has no access to your vault, your master password or your use of the app. Data leaves your phone only when you yourself choose to back it up, export or share it.

3. What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer receives no passwords, vault contents, account names, usage statistics, device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses or location from the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, tracking or crash-reporting code and sends no such information anywhere. Google Play, which distributes the app, may show the developer aggregated and anonymous app-quality statistics (for example install counts or crash rates) under Google's own terms and privacy policy; these cannot identify you and give no access to your vault.

4. Information that stays on your phone

  • Vault data: entries (titles, user names, passwords, websites, notes, one-time code secrets, custom fields, categories), password history, attachments and settings. The vault is stored in a database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). Its key is derived from your master password with Argon2id.
  • Your master password is never stored and never leaves the phone. If you forget it, it cannot be recovered by anyone.
  • Biometric unlock (optional): Android protects a key in the Android Keystore and releases it after Android accepts your fingerprint or face. CipherON never receives biometric data.
  • App settings and sign-in tokens for optional services are kept in the app's private storage; secrets among them are encrypted with a Keystore key.
  • Clipboard: passwords and codes you copy are marked as sensitive and cleared automatically after the delay you set. Other apps with clipboard access on your phone may read the clipboard while it holds them.
  • Screenshots and recent-apps previews are blocked by default; you can allow them in Settings. The optional encrypted file storage described in section 18 always blocks them while it is open.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app, so the vault is not copied to Google or to a new phone by those features.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: only when you tap Scan QR or scan text. Frames are processed on the phone (including on-device text recognition) and are neither stored nor sent anywhere.
  • Photos and files: only the items you pick (for example a QR code image, an attachment, a backup file, or the files and folders you choose to lock in the optional encrypted file storage) are read, for that action only. When you lock files there, the app asks Android to remove the originals, and Android may ask you to confirm. The app does not scan or list your other files.
  • Biometric: only to offer fingerprint or face unlock if you turn it on.
  • Internet and network state: only for the optional Google Drive backup and SMB sync you set up.

6. Logos

CipherON shows a small logo next to an entry when the entry's title, website or user name matches a well-known service. The logos come from public sources (open icon collections and Wikimedia Commons) and are used for one purpose only: to help you identify your own accounts at a glance. They are not used for advertising, promotion or any other purpose, and their use does not mean any company endorses CipherON or is connected with it. All logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners. The logos are bundled inside the app and matched on your phone; nothing is downloaded and no entry, title or website is sent to any server for this. Credits are in Settings, About, and in the Third-Party Notices.

7. OPTIONAL: GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP

If you tap Sign in with Google, you sign in on Google's own page in your browser; CipherON never sees your Google password. The app requests one scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which lets it view and manage only the files and folders CipherON itself creates in your Drive. It cannot see any other file.

What it does: creates a folder named CipherON, uploads backup files there (your vault in already-encrypted form, readable only with your master password), lists them so you can restore one, downloads the one you pick, and deletes old backups according to your "backups to keep" setting. It also reads the email address of the signed-in account to show which account is connected.

This information goes only between your phone and Google. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not used for any purpose other than the backup and restore you asked for. Google processes the files under its own terms and privacy policy.

To disconnect, press Sign out in CipherON and, to revoke access at Google too, remove CipherON at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You may delete the CipherON folder from your Drive at any time.

CipherON's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. OPTIONAL: SMB SYNC

If you enter an SMB file-share address and login, CipherON uploads the encrypted vault file to the server you choose and control. The server's operator can see an encrypted file but not its contents. The login password is stored encrypted on your phone. The developer has no access to your server.

9. Files you export or share

If you export to plain CSV, Excel or PDF, or share a backup, the file is written where you choose and leaves the app's protection. The same applies to files you send out of the optional encrypted file storage: they are written to your phone's Pictures, Movies, Music or Downloads folder as ordinary, unprotected files. Plain exports are not encrypted unless you select the encrypted option. Protect those files yourself and delete them when no longer needed.

10. Sharing and disclosure

The developer holds no personal data from the app and therefore has nothing to share, sell, rent or disclose, and does not do so. Because the developer holds no copy of your vault, the developer cannot hand it over to anyone and cannot recover it.

11. Retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as you keep it. You can edit or delete entries, delete the whole vault from Settings, remove backups from your Drive or share, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. Copies you backed up or exported are under your control.

12. Security

The app uses AES-256 encryption, Argon2id key derivation, the Android Keystore, screens protected against capture by default, and automatic locking. No system can be made perfectly secure; keep your device updated, choose a strong master password and keep your own backups.

13. Your rights

Your data is on your device and under your control, so you can access, correct, export and delete it yourself in the app. Privacy laws that may apply to you (for example the EU and UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) give rights over personal data that a controller holds. The developer does not hold or process your personal data, so there is nothing for the developer to disclose, correct or erase. You may still contact the developer with any question or request, and nothing in this policy limits rights that the law gives you.

14. Children

CipherON is not directed at children and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Children should use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

15. International use

The app works on your device wherever you are. If you choose Google Drive or an SMB server, your encrypted files are handled by those services in the places they operate.

16. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at https://kssp-apps.github.io/cipheron-site/privacy.html and included in the next app update, with a new effective date. Material changes will be described in the update notes.

17. Website contact form

The CipherON website has a contact form. It does not send anything by itself: when you press Send it opens your own email app with a message addressed to the developer, already filled in with what you typed. The developer receives only what you choose to send, uses it only to reply to you, and keeps it only as long as needed for that purpose. This applies only to messages you send to the developer; it is separate from the app, which collects nothing.

18. Optional encrypted file storage

CipherON includes an optional feature that lets you lock files and folders you choose, such as pictures, videos and documents, in encrypted storage inside the app. It is protected by a separate password that you create. It is not needed to use the password vault.

What it does: when you pick files or a folder with Android's file picker, the app encrypts a copy in its private storage, checks the copy, and then asks Android to remove the originals, so they no longer appear in the Gallery or the Files app. When you send files out, the app decrypts them, writes them back to your phone's Pictures, Movies, Music or Downloads folder, where they appear again, and removes them from the encrypted storage.

How it is protected: every file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a random key. That key is protected by your separate password with Argon2id. File and folder names are encrypted too. The key is held in memory only while the storage is open; it is discarded when you lock the vault, leave the app or close the storage. Screenshots are blocked while it is open. After several wrong password tries the app makes you wait before you can try again.

Your separate password is never stored or sent anywhere and cannot be recovered or reset by anyone, including the developer. If you forget it, the files inside are permanently lost.

Where it is kept: when you create it you choose either a folder on your phone (the app makes a hidden folder named .cipheron inside the folder you pick) or the app's own private storage. The files in that folder are already encrypted and have random names. They stay there when you uninstall the app, and a new install can use them again if you point it to the same folder and know the vault password. Anyone who can reach that folder, such as another app with storage access or someone using a computer, can copy the encrypted files but cannot read them without your vault password; deleting them from the folder deletes the locked files for good.

Locked files never leave your phone through CipherON. They are not uploaded, and they are not part of Google Drive backups, SMB sync, exports or Android backup. The developer receives nothing from this feature.

This storage is independent of the password vault: deleting the password vault from Settings, starting a new one after forgetting the master password, or self-destruction after too many wrong master password tries does not erase it. You remove files from it yourself by deleting them or sending them out. Uninstalling the app removes a vault kept in the app's private storage for good; a vault kept in a folder you chose stays in that folder until you delete it.

Limits: files you send out are ordinary, unprotected files. Copies made earlier by you or by other apps, for example automatic photo backups, are not affected by locking a file. Keep your own backups: the developer cannot recover locked files.
